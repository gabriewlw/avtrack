Technical work gets harder when the information is somewhere else.

AVTrack was conceived by a cruise ship broadcast technician to replace scattered spreadsheets, notes, and recovery folders with one practical source of truth.

It is made for the moment a screen goes dark, a device needs configuration, an inventory must be verified, or a known-good file has to be restored quickly.

02 ONE WORKSPACE · FOUR JOBS

Built around the way
technical teams actually work.

AV DEVICES

Reach the right device before the pressure builds.

Search by device, venue, IP, VLAN, or system. Copy an address instantly and keep a visible record of what your team has already confirmed.

  • Fast IP access
  • Venue and system filters
  • Manual reachability confirmation
IPTV CHANNELS

Keep every channel endpoint ready to test.

Document onboard and satellite streams with the address, UDP port, source, and notes needed to investigate a missing channel without searching through old sheets.

  • Complete UDP endpoints
  • Onboard and satellite groups
  • Searchable technical notes
EQUIPMENT INVENTORY

Turn a repetitive stock check into a clear workflow.

Paste from Excel, map custom columns, review by location, flag missing information, and confirm multiple items while you are physically in the locker.

  • Flexible inventory templates
  • Location-based inspection
  • Dated stock-check history
AV BACKUPS

Keep recovery files close to the equipment they protect.

Store known-good configurations for switchers, routers, receivers, and production systems with a clear device name, date, type, and recovery note.

  • Normal local files
  • Equipment and version context
  • Ready for emergency recovery
